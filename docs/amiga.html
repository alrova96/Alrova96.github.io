The Project

GHG Flux Measurements

Simultaneous CH₄ and CO₂ flux measurements using LI-COR gas analyzers and smart chamber systems, combined with drone-mounted Sniffer 4D sensors for spatially resolved atmospheric sampling over the Emperor penguin colony at Atka Bay.

Ice Shelf Dynamics

Understanding how ice shelf dynamics affect greenhouse gas emissions. Laboratory analyses of ice cores and meltwater from the Atka and Ekström ice shelves will identify biogeochemical drivers of gas exchange.

AI-Driven Upscaling

Convolutional neural networks for automated penguin counts and machine learning algorithms integrating drone multispectral data with Sentinel-2 satellite imagery for regional scaling of site measurements.

Penguin Colony Assessment

Guano analysis and population assessment of the Emperor penguin colony to understand biological methane hotspots and their role in Antarctic carbon cycles, supporting global GHG budget assessments.

Drone Remote Sensing & Open Data

Drone-based multispectral, thermal, and LiDAR imaging to assess surface properties. All datasets and algorithms published as open-access resources for future polar research.
